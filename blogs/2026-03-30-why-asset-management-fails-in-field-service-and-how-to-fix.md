Effective asset management depends on accurate, real-time information from the field. This article explores how mobile ERP tools, offline capabilities, barcode and RFID scanning, and proactive maintenance strategies help organisations reduce siloes, improve asset visibility, and connect back-office teams with work happening on the ground.
