Rising fuel prices are putting pressure on organisations with dispersed field teams, but the real opportunity lies in controlling fuel usage. This article explores how smarter scheduling, route optimisation, fleet visibility, and integrated work management can help reduce unnecessary travel, lower operational costs, and improve field service efficiency.
