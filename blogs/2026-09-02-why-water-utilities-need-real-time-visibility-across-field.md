Water utilities manage some of the most essential services in any city. When infrastructure fails, the impact is immediate: residents lose access to water, contact centres come under pressure, field teams need to respond quickly, and operational teams must coordinate repairs across complex networks. Yet many water utilities still rely on outdated, manual processes to manage outage response, maintenance work, material usage, inspections and customer communication.
